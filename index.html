Which bag types can I enquire about?

You can enquire about D-cut, W-cut, loop-handle, box, bottom gusset and shoe bags. If you are unsure which format fits your products, share the intended use and ask for guidance.

Yes. Tell us your preferred size, GSM and colour, or choose “Need guidance” in the quote form. The suitable specification can then be discussed for your requirement.

Yes. Share your logo, artwork or brand colours through WhatsApp and discuss logo placement or multicolour customized printing for your bag.

These depend on the bag format, size, GSM, printing requirement and delivery location. Share your details so the suitable order quantity and timing can be confirmed with your quote.
